Privacy Notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Purpose and scope

This is a private, non-commercial integration for the application owner's own ING bank accounts. It uses Enable Banking to retrieve account and transaction information for personal budgeting in a self-hosted instance of Actual Budget. It is not offered to other users.

Information processed

With the account holder's permission, the integration may process account identifiers, account balances, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions and counterparties, to the extent supplied by the bank. These records are used for budget management and categorisation.

Where information goes

ING provides account information through Enable Banking. The resulting budgeting information is processed in the owner's self-hosted Actual Budget environment. Enable Banking and the bank operate under their own applicable privacy notices. The operator does not intentionally publish bank-account details or transaction data on this public website.

Access and security

Access to the Actual Budget installation is restricted to the owner and authorised household access. Bank access is limited to the permissions granted by the account holder. Consent can be revoked via the bank's consent-management facilities.

Retention

Financial records are retained for the owner's personal budgeting purposes and can be deleted by the owner from the budgeting system. Technical records at Enable Banking, ING and GitHub are governed by those providers' respective policies.

This website

These informational pages do not intentionally use advertising trackers or collect banking data. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical access information in accordance with GitHub's privacy practices.

Privacy enquiries

The application owner can be contacted through the data-protection contact email supplied during Enable Banking application registration.

This notice describes personal use; it is not a representation that these static pages operate a banking service.
